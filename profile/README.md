## What is this?

Welcome! This is the home of the **AI & Water Community of Practice** — a space for anyone working on, thinking about, or curious about the use of AI and data science in the water sector to connect, share, and build things together.

Whether you're deploying models operationally, wrestling with the same data quality headaches everyone else has, or just want to know what "good" looks like before you commit to a project, this community is for you.

We're bringing together people from across the sector — water companies, the Environment Agency, research institutes, consultancies, and academia — to swap experience, avoid duplicated effort, and (hopefully) raise the bar for how AI gets used in water management across the UK.

## Why does it exist?

AI in the water sector is moving fast, but a lot of the learning is happening in silos. This community exists to:

- Share real operational experience — what's worked, what hasn't, and why
- Build shared understanding of good practice, standards, and pitfalls
- Give people a place to ask questions without having to reinvent the wheel
- Create space for cross-sector collaboration on shared challenges
- Provide a bridge between research and operational deployment

## Who's involved

This community is convened through the **Manchester Environmental Research Institute (MERI)**, with the **Environment Agency** as a founding participant. But it's built to grow — the more perspectives we have from across the sector, the more useful it becomes.

## How to get involved

You don't need to be an AI expert, and you don't need permission to show up.

- **Browse and comment** — everything here is open. Look around, read the working documents, see what's being discussed.
- **Join the conversation** — head to the [Discussions](../../discussions) tab to ask a question, share something you've learned, or say hello. You just need a free GitHub account — no need to formally join anything.
- **Contribute a document** — working documents (meeting notes, guidance, terms of reference) live in [`/docs`](./docs). Suggest an edit via a pull request, or open an issue if you'd rather flag something for someone else to add.
- **Share code or tools** — if you've got something to share (a model, a script, a package), let us know via Discussions and we'll set it up as its own repository in this organisation, so it gets proper versioning and its own home.
- **Request to join as a member** — if you'd like to be a formal member of the organisation (useful if you want to contribute regularly), just [open an issue](../../issues/new) or email us at *[placeholder: contact email]* and we'll add you.
